METEOROLOGICAL INPUTS
Ta, RH, u, Fsd, Fld, SWC   + measured Rn (for G and ΔQc)
half-hourly tower record (Table 1) · observed fluxes for calibration and evaluation (EddyPro v7.0.9, PyFluxPro v3.4)
↓
INITIALIZE SURFACE TEMPERATURE
Ts = Ta + 3 K
first guess for the iterative solver
↓
NET RADIATION
Rn = (1 − α)Fsd + εFld − εσTs4
α(t) = αnoon + Δα·[1 − cos(π|t − 12|/6)],   αnoon = 0.098,  Δα = 0.0065
gray body: ε = 0.97 for the absorbed and the emitted longwave radiation
↓
ELECTRICITY GENERATION
Eelec = ηFsd,   η = 0.11   (Fsd > 50 W m−2)
energy exported off-site as electricity, removed from the local heat budget
↓
AERODYNAMIC RESISTANCE  ra
ra,n = [ln((z − d)/z0m)·ln((z − d)/z0h)]/(κ2u)
ra = ra,n · fseas · fstab · fRH · fm,   fm = 1.57
fseas: 1.00 May–Sep, 1.02 Apr and Oct, 1.05 Dec–Feb, 1.08 Mar and Nov
↓
CANOPY RESISTANCE  rc  (JARVIS–STEWART)
rc = rc,base · fseas(rc) · fTa · fRH(rc) · fSWC · fVPD · fFsd,   30 ≤ rc ≤ 800 s m−1
rc,base (full record): MAM 580, JJA 596, SON 591, DJF 649 s m−1
fseas(rc): 0.85 Jun–Aug, 1.20 Sep, 1.10 May and Oct, 0.90 Mar and Nov, 1.00 Apr and Dec–Feb
↓
GROUND HEAT & FOUNDATION STORAGE
Gsurface = Gplate + Cs(SWC)·Δz·(dTsoil/dt),   Δz = 0.10 m
Cs(SWC) = (1 − φ)Cm + SWC·Cw + (φ − SWC)Ca,   φ = 0.44
G = g1Rn + g2(dRn/dt) + g3   (hysteresis form, per season)
ΔQc = c1Rn + c2(dRn/dt) + c3,   c1 = 0.0430, c2 = 131 s, c3 = −4.3 W m−2
Rn and dRn/dt measured · G fitted to Gsurface · ΔQc: concrete-pile heat storage (OHM)
↓
TURBULENT HEAT FLUXES
H = ρcp(Ts − Ta)/ra
LE = ρLv[qs(Ts) − qa]/(ra + rc)
rc is bypassed (ra only) when qs(Ts) < qa (dew or condensation)
↓
SURFACE-TEMPERATURE SOLVER  (NEWTON–RAPHSON)
Res(Ts) = Rn − H − LE − G − Eelec − ΔQc,   Ts ← Ts − Res/(dRes/dTs)
with the updated Ts, repeat from NET RADIATION until |Res| < 0.1 W m−2 (at most 50 iterations)
↓
OUTPUTS
Rn = H + LE + G + Eelec + ΔQc  (solver solution)      β = H/LE
diurnal residual correction of H and LE, added after the solution: ΔF = aF sin θ + bF cos θ + cF,  θ = (t − 12)π/6
F = H, LE; reported flux F + ΔF
five energy terms and the Bowen ratio at every half-hourly step
↓
CALIBRATION & VALIDATION
season-stratified five-fold cross-validation (rc,base and diurnal correction refitted in each fold)
fixed once: αnoon, Δα, g1–g3, fm (full record); c1–c3 (pile temperature record)
benchmarked against principal-component regression (PCR) and a convolutional neural network (CNN)
independent hold-out test: training 1 May 2024 to 30 April 2025, test 1 May 2025 to 1 February 2026
R2 (cross-validation / hold-out): H 0.90 / 0.91 · LE 0.64 / 0.65 · G 0.81 / 0.81
